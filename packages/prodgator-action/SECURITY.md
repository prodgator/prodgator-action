# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@prodgator.io**. Include a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and the URL, endpoint, or component involved. Do not open a public GitHub issue for a security report.

Full policy, scope, and safe harbor terms: https://docs.prodgator.io/security/disclosure

Also published as [security.txt](https://app.prodgator.io/.well-known/security.txt) (RFC 9116).

## What to expect

We acknowledge new reports within 3 business days, keep you updated as we investigate, and credit you on request once the issue is fixed. Good-faith research that follows the policy above is covered by our safe harbor terms. We do not run a paid bug bounty program right now.
